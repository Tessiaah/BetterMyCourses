# BetterMyCourses

A more comfortable [Aalto MyCourses](https://mycourses.aalto.fi/), with a true-black theme, personal colors, custom banners and study tools.

BetterMyCourses is a browser extension for **Brave, Chrome and Edge**. It makes courses, quizzes, grades and announcements easier to read while keeping the familiar MyCourses layout.

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/Tessiaah/BetterMyCourses/releases/latest)

## Make MyCourses yours

- **True-black dark theme** with softer text, readable tables and clearer grade reports.
- **Personal link colors** with Champagne, Ivory, Sage and custom options.
- **Custom Home and Dashboard banners** with your own images, adjustable framing and zoom, and a preview that matches your open page.
- **Cleaner navigation** with consistent course tabs, comfortable spacing and clearer announcements.
- **Easier quizzes** with visible answer fields, readable diagrams, a clearer timer and green, yellow or red feedback based on the site's grading.
- **Quick theme controls** to switch between BetterMyCourses and the original appearance.

## Study Assist

An optional workspace available from the quiz side panel, with drawing tools and a personal formula library.

### Drawing

Sketch over the page to work through a calculation or annotate a diagram. Choose a pen color and thickness, use the eraser, undo a stroke or clear your drawings. Browse mode lets you interact with the page while keeping your notes visible. Closing Study Assist clears the drawings.

### Formulas

Keep useful formulas within reach. Browse starter references for Calculus and Electrical Engineering, or build your own collection:

- Add and manage subjects.
- Save formulas with a title and LaTeX, with a live preview.
- Search formula titles across all subjects.
- Edit or delete entries as your collection grows.
- Click a formula to place it on the page, then drag it wherever it helps.

Placed formulas stay visible when you close Study Assist. Remove each one with its **X** button. Your saved formula collection remains available for future study sessions.

## Get started

Download and extract the ZIP from the [latest release](https://github.com/Tessiaah/BetterMyCourses/releases/latest). On your browser's Extensions page, enable **Developer mode**, choose **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder. Open MyCourses, choose your colors and banners, and enable Study Assist whenever you want it.
